# Jacob Design Studio print standards

The reference standard is the Swiss editorial grid tradition (Müller-Brockmann, Ruder, Tschichold) as practised in premium corporate books: a fixed grid, a baseline, one type family, one accent, and absolute consistency across pages.

## Format
- A4: 210 x 297 mm trim, 216 x 303 mm page with 3 mm bleed.
- Live area: 174 mm wide (x 21 to 195), 255 mm tall (y 24 to 279).

## Grid
- 12 columns, 10.83 mm each, 4 mm gutters.
- Column starts: x = 21 + n * 14.83 mm.
- Every image, text block, rule and caption starts on a column edge.

## Baseline and type
- 4.5 mm baseline. Blocks start at 24, 28.5, 33, 37.5 mm and so on.
- Display 40 to 44 pt on 42 pt leading.
- Headline 24 to 28 pt on 30 pt.
- Body 9 to 10 pt on 15 pt.
- Caption 7 to 7.5 pt on 10.5 pt.
- Label 6.5 pt, tracked 0.16 em.
- One body size per page.

## Alignment
- Body and captions flush left, ragged right.
- Captions align to the left edge of their photograph.
- Folio at the right edge of column 12, 9 mm above trim, same place on every page.
- Rules span column 1 to 12, or 1 to 6. No eyeballed widths.
- No centred paragraph. No floating blocks in margins.

## Page system
- Every page: header, main block, secondary block, folio in the same grid positions.
- Cover: photograph at full column width or full bleed, brand plate 55 to 100 mm, title on the grid.
- Story: one idea, one dominant picture, one headline, one short text block.
- Plate: whole picture, callouts on the nearest edges, palette, material index.
- Closing: one contact plate, closing line, Tamil line, motif, folio.

## Enforcement
- The designer prompt carries these numbers.
- The per page layout inspector rejects overlaps, clipped text, empty bands, misalignment and floating blocks.
- The art director judge scores the book against the grid and alignment law.
