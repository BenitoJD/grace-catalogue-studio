Fig. 2 · walk-in wardrobe, {{room}}
From the workshop
Square carcass, scribed to the wall.
The carcass is built square and scribed to the wall, so the shutters hang true even where the plaster does not. Hinges are set after the finish cures, then adjusted once the room is in use. Every drawer is run through its full travel twice before the wardrobe leaves the bench. The shutters are offered up dry first, then taken down for finishing; no silica and no filler strip is used where a scribe will do. Site measurements are taken with the skirting in place, and the ceiling line is checked at both ends of the run. Delivery follows the flooring, and the fitting team sets the reveals before the room is handed back.
